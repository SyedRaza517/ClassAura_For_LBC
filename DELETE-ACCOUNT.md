# Delete your account — EdTech Hub

**App:** EdTech Hub
**Developer / provider:** EdTech

This page explains how to request deletion of your **EdTech Hub** account and what happens to your data. Last updated: 5 August 2026.

---

## How to delete your account

### If you are a member of staff

1. Open **EdTech Hub** and sign in.
2. Tap **More** on the home screen.
3. Tap **Delete my account**.
4. Enter your password to confirm. If you have an authenticator app enabled, enter your six-digit code as well.
5. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

### If you are a student

1. Open **EdTech Hub** and sign in.
2. Tap **More**.
3. Tap **Delete my account**.
4. Enter your password to confirm.
5. Confirm. Your sign-in is removed immediately.

### If you cannot sign in

Email **info@edtech.co.uk** from the address on your account, with the subject **"Delete my Staff Hub account"**. We will verify your identity with the College and complete the deletion within **30 days**.

---

## What is deleted

### Staff accounts

Deleting your account permanently removes:

- Your profile — name, email address, job title and department
- Your password and any two-factor authentication settings
- Your daily check-in history and daily summaries
- Your holiday requests and holiday balance adjustments
- Your timesheets and the sessions recorded on them
- Your performance and self-reflection review records
- Your in-app notifications
- Your push-notification device tokens
- Any outstanding password-reset links
- Any sign-up request holding the same email address

### Student accounts

Deleting your account removes your **sign-in access and login data** — your password and any active sessions. You will no longer be able to open the app.

---

## What is kept, and why

- **Your academic record (students).** Attendance, assessment marks and enrolment are the College's official student records and are held independently of the app. They are **not** deleted when you remove your app account, and are retained by the College under its own records-retention policy. To ask about these, contact the College directly.

- **Student progress reviews written by staff.** If you are a member of staff and you recorded progress conversations about students, those records remain part of the student's file. Your personal account is deleted, but your name stays on reviews you wrote so the student's record still shows who wrote it.

- **College documents.** Policies and forms shared with you belong to the College. They are detached from your account rather than destroyed.

- **Administrator notification.** College administrators are told that an account was deleted, including the name and email of the account, so that staffing and access records stay accurate.

---

## Retention period

Deletion from the live system is **immediate**. Encrypted backups held by our database provider may contain a copy for a short period — typically up to **30 days** — before being cycled out. No further copies are kept.

Records the College retains as official student or employment records (described above) are held under the College's own retention policy and are not affected by app account deletion.

---

## One limitation to be aware of

If you are the **only administrator**, the app will not let you delete your account. Removing the last administrator would leave nobody able to approve leave, take registers or manage staff, with no way to appoint a replacement. Appoint another administrator first, then delete your account.
