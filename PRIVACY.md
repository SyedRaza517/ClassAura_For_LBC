# Privacy Policy

**EdTech — EdTech Hub**
Last updated: 5 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the **EdTech Hub** application ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores and protects personal data when you use it. The app is provided by **EdTech** ("the College") for use by its **staff, administrators and enrolled students**. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data in line with the **UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)** and the Data Protection Act 2018.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is **EdTech**. For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at **info@edtech.co.uk**.

## 2. What data we collect

**For all users (staff and students):**

- **Account details** — your name and email address.
- **Authentication data** — a securely hashed password and, where enabled for staff, two-step (authenticator) verification data.
- **Device data** — if you enable push notifications, a device notification token; and standard technical data needed to operate the service securely.
- **Messages you send** — the content of queries and messages you submit through the app.

**For staff and administrators, additionally:**

- **Employment details** — job title, department and home site.
- **Attendance & leave** — daily check-in/out times, daily summaries, holiday balances and leave requests (including the reason you provide).
- **Documents & notifications** — documents shared with you and in-app notifications.
- **Timesheets** — the teaching sessions you record: date, start and end time, duration, whether it was on campus or online, and the title or description you give each session.
- **Performance and reflection records** — your answers to the College's review forms: the Strategic Lecturer Self-Reflection you complete about yourself, and reviews a manager completes about you, together with the term or month each relates to.
- **Student progress reviews you write** — the record of a progress conversation with a student: your assessment of their progress, any concerns, what was agreed and whether a follow-up is needed. Your name is stored on the review as its author.
- **Administrative records (admin users only)** — where you hold an administrator role, the app processes student records (names, student reference / College ID numbers, college email addresses), attendance registers, assessment marks and pastoral (PAT) interaction notes, for the purpose of running the College's programmes.

**For students, additionally:**

- **Student identifiers** — first and last name, your **College ID / student reference number**, and your college email address.
- **Academic records** — your attendance by unit and your assessment results/grades, shown to you within the app.
- **Queries to the College** — the subject and content of queries you send to administrators, and the responses sent back to you.
- **Progress reviews about you** — records written by your lecturers of progress conversations with you: how you are getting on, any concerns raised, what was agreed and whether a follow-up is planned. These are visible to you in the app, to the lecturer who wrote them, and to College administrators.

## 3. How we use your data

- To provide the app's features: sign-in; for staff — attendance, leave management, timesheets, documents, reviews and notifications, and (for administrators) student attendance and assessment management; for students — viewing your attendance, assessment results and progress reviews, and contacting the College.
- To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
- To send you notifications relevant to your account (e.g. leave decisions, responses to your queries).
- To review and approve new account requests submitted through the app.

## 4. Legal basis for processing

We process your data on the basis of the College's **legitimate interests** in administering its staff and educational programmes, to perform our contract with you as a member of staff, and to provide educational services to enrolled students. Special-category or sensitive information is only processed where necessary and with appropriate safeguards.

## 5. How your data is stored and protected

- Data is stored in a **PostgreSQL database hosted on Supabase** within the **European Union**.
- All communication between the app and our servers is **encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)**.
- Passwords are **securely hashed** and never stored in plain text.
- Access is protected by signed session tokens, role-based access control, per-account data isolation (users only see their own records), and optional two-step verification for staff.

## 6. Sharing your data

The app contains **no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking software**. We do **not** sell your personal data, we do **not** use it for advertising, and we do **not** profile you.

We share data only with service providers that host and operate the app on our behalf, under appropriate data-protection terms:

- **Supabase** — database hosting (EU).
- **Render** — application/API hosting.
- **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** — only if push notifications are enabled, to deliver notifications to your device.
- **Resend** — email delivery, for account invitations, password-reset links and security notifications.

## 6a. Data we import from the College's learning environment

To keep records consistent, the app imports data from the College's own Moodle virtual learning environment (VLE): course and unit structures, enrolled students and assessment marks entered by teaching staff. This is a one-way import from a College system into the app. No personal data is sent from the app back to the VLE, and no data is shared with any party outside the College as part of this process.

## 7. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as you hold an account with the College, and delete or anonymise it when it is no longer needed, or when you delete your account (see below), subject to any legal or academic retention obligations (for example, the College may retain academic records where required).

## 8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to data portability. To exercise these rights, contact **info@edtech.co.uk**.

You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).

## 9. Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your own app account from within the app:

- **Staff:** Staff App → More → Delete my account. This removes your profile, check-in history, leave requests, balance adjustments, notifications and any outstanding password-reset links.
- **Students:** Student App → More → Delete account. This removes your app sign-in access and personal login data. Your underlying academic record held by the College (attendance and assessment results) may be retained by the College as part of its official student records, independently of the app.

Deleting your account requires your password and cannot be undone.

## 10. Children

The app is intended for College staff, administrators and enrolled students. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 through this app.
